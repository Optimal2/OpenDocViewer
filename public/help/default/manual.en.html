Getting started with OpenDocViewer

OpenDocViewer (ODV) is the window where you read documents when you open them from your organization's portal or archive system. This guide shows what you need day to day: finding the right page, zooming, and printing. It takes a few minutes to read.

Want to do more – select pages, compare two pages, or see every keyboard shortcut? Two more guides cover that, and you can expand them at the bottom of this page. The pictures show a made-up test document with no real data. The screenshots show the Swedish interface; choose English in the Language menu to get the button names used in this guide. 1. What it looks like

ODV has three parts: the toolbar at the top, the thumbnail pane on the left and the document in the middle. The page you are looking at has a blue frame.

Three words you will see again: a document is a file with one or more pages. A session is all the documents you opened together from the portal or archive system. The thumbnails are grouped by document (DOK 1, DOK 2 … in the Swedish interface).

Back to the portal

ODV is opened from another system – a portal, an archive or a business application. You get back there with that system's own buttons or by closing the ODV tab. What it looks like depends on your organization.

2. Wait until everything has loaded

When you have opened the documents, ODV fetches the pages. Meanwhile the page counter is striped and the print and selection buttons are grey. When the stripes disappear, everything is ready.

Why is the light by the print button off?

The small light by the print button belongs to a quick-print feature that your organization may have turned off. If it is off, that is normal. The light on the help button to the right, on the other hand, shows how ODV is doing: green is good, yellow means the computer is low on memory, red means an error.

3. Find the right page

There are four simple ways, and you can mix them as you like.

  1. The arrow buttons in the toolbar: first, previous, next and last page.
  2. Type the page number in the box between the arrows and press Enter.
  3. Click a thumbnail in the left pane.
  4. Scroll with the mouse wheel. When you reach the bottom edge of the page and keep scrolling, a small gauge fills up – and the next page is shown. The same works upwards.
More ways to browse
  • Arrow Down / Arrow Up or Page Down / Page Up change page. Hold the key down to browse quickly.
  • Home and End go to the first and last page.
  • Hold down the previous or next button in the toolbar to browse quickly.
  • Hold Ctrl and the arrow buttons jump a whole document instead of one page. You can tell because the page navigation gets a dashed frame.

4. Zoom and fit

The zoom buttons are in the middle of the toolbar.

  • The magnifying glasses (Zoom out, Zoom in) zoom out and in. You can also type a percentage in the box between them.
  • 1:1 shows the page at its actual size.
  • Fit to screen shows the whole page at once.
  • Fit to width fills the width – it suits most documents. The button is red when it is selected.
More ways to zoom
  • + and - on the keyboard zoom in and out.
  • The 1:X button shows the page at a size you have chosen yourself, and HQ makes a PDF page even sharper. Both are described in the guide More features at the bottom of the page.

5. Rotate, lighter or darker

Is the page sideways, or is a scanned page too dark or too faint? The buttons on the right of the toolbar help you.

  • Rotate left 90° and Rotate right 90° turn the page a quarter turn.
  • Brightness and Contrast each open a slider. Drag it, or click on the scale.
  • Reset adjustments, next to the sliders, brings brightness and contrast back to normal.

The changes are temporary. Rotation, brightness and contrast only change how the page is shown to you right now. Nothing is saved in the archive, and the next time the document is opened it looks as usual again.

On the printout the changes are included only if you choose the print method Active page – both when you are looking at one page and when you compare two. Every other print method (All pages, Simple range, Custom pages and printing a selection) prints the pages as they are in the archive, without your changes.

Rotate with the keyboard

Ctrl+Arrow Left and Ctrl+Arrow Right rotate the page a quarter turn, just like the buttons.

6. The thumbnail pane

The pane on the left shows every page as a small picture, grouped by document. Click a thumbnail to go there. The buttons at the top of the pane make it wider or narrower, and the crossed-out eye hides it completely.

Every thumbnail has small badges in its corners (the letters come from the Swedish interface):

T – the page's place in the whole session D – the document number S – the page number within the document
Right-clicking a thumbnail

Right-click a thumbnail to get a small menu: show the document's metadata (for example ID, department and date – which fields exist depends on the system that opened the documents), hide the page or the whole document, or show the page on the right in compare mode. Hiding pages is called selection and has its own guide: Selection – choose the pages that are shown and printed.

7. Printing

This is how you print the page you are looking at, or all pages.

  1. Click the print button at the far left of the toolbar (or press Ctrl+P).
  2. Choose Active page or All pages under Print method. Have you rotated the page or changed brightness and contrast? Then only Active page puts that on paper.
  3. Fill in Reason and Recipient if the dialog asks for them. Your organization decides which fields exist and which are required.
  4. If the Add copy watermark option is there, leave it ticked if the printout is not an original.
  5. Click Print. ODV prepares the pages and then opens the browser's print window, where you choose a printer as usual.

Active page prints the page exactly as you see it – with rotation, brightness and contrast if you changed them. All pages and the other methods print the pages as they are in the archive, without such changes.

The printout can automatically get a header and a footer with information your organization has chosen – for example date, user, document ID, reason and page number – and a watermark if you left it ticked.

More print options
  • The arrow next to the print button decides whether the dialog opens with Active page or All pages preselected. ODV remembers your choice.
  • Simple range prints from one page to another, for example 2–10. Custom pages takes a list, for example 1-3, 7, 9-5. The order you type is the order they are printed in.
  • Automatic page orientation lets ODV choose portrait or landscape per page. Untick it if everything should be portrait.
  • Continue working moves the preparation box to the bottom so you can keep reading. When it is ready, click Open print.
  • After your first printout, the Reuse latest print settings button appears at the top right of the dialog. It fills in the same choices as last time.
  • Want to print only some pages from several documents? Then make a selection first – see the guide Selection at the bottom of the page.

8. Theme, language and help

At the far right of the toolbar there are three menus. Theme chooses a light or dark look, Language switches between Swedish and English, and Help opens this guide. ODV remembers your choices until next time.

9. If something goes wrong

The pages are shown as warning triangles

The document links from the portal or archive system may be valid for a limited time. If you have had ODV open for a long time, the pages may stop showing and a box appears at the top. Click Reset session and the documents are fetched again. You do not need to reload the browser.

Other common situations

  • The print button is grey. The pages are still loading – wait until the page counter is no longer striped.
  • Pages are missing from the thumbnail pane. A selection is active (the selection button in the toolbar is red). Click the red button at the top of the pane to show all pages again.
  • The page is blurry. If it is a PDF page, the HQ button can make it sharper. Scanned pages are already shown as sharp as they are.
If you need to contact support

Contact your local support, as usual in your organization. Open the help menu and choose About OpenDocViewer. It shows the version, and under Diagnostics you can download a file to attach to the ticket. The file contains technical information about ODV – no documents. If the expired-session box is shown, you can expand Technical details there too. More about this in More features.

Selection – choose the pages that are shown and printed

Important: a selection hides pages in ODV and controls what is printed. The original documents in the archive are never affected.

1. Open the selection workspace 2. Remove pages 3. Add pages back 4. Change the order 5. Take a closer look at a page 6. Save or leave 7. How to tell that a selection is active 8. Print the selection 9. Remove the selection

1. Open the selection workspace

Click the selection button in the toolbar – the one with a list and a tick, directly to the right of the print button (tooltip: Manage the pages shown and printed). The Manage selection workspace opens over the whole window.

Two modes: Documents and Pages
  • Documents is the default. Pages are kept together per document. You can remove and add pages and move them within their document.
  • Pages drops the grouping. Pages from different documents can be mixed in any order you like – for example before printing in a particular order.

2. Remove pages

  1. Mark the pages in the right panel that should not be included. Click one page at a time.
  2. Click Remove selected – the arrow ‹ between the panels. The pages lose their tick in the left panel.
  3. To remove everything and start from scratch: click Remove all («).
Faster ways to mark and remove
  • Shift+click marks every page between two clicks. Ctrl+click adds or removes a single page from the marking.
  • Drag a rectangle over several pages to mark them all at once.
  • Double-click a page in the left panel to add or remove it directly.
  • You do not need to open the workspace at all for single pages: right-click a thumbnail or the page in the main view and choose Hide this page or Hide document. The Delete key hides the page you are looking at, Ctrl+Delete the whole document.

3. Add pages back

Changed your mind? Mark the pages in the left panel and click Add selected (›), or Add all (») to get everything back. The Undo and Redo buttons in the toolbar take back one step at a time.

4. Change the order

Drag a page in the right panel to a new place. In Documents mode you can move pages within their document; in Pages mode you can mix freely. The order in the selection becomes the order of the printout.

5. Take a closer look at a page

Hover over a page and click the eye. The page is shown large, with arrows to browse to the next one. A green edge means the page is included in the selection, and the button at the top adds or removes the page directly. Esc closes.

6. Save or leave

The Save button turns green as soon as you have changed something. Click it when you are done, and then Leave to get back to the document. If you leave without saving, you get a question.

7. How to tell that a selection is active

Back in the document, the selection button is red, the page counter only counts the selection's pages, and the thumbnail pane only shows the pages that are included. At the top of the pane there is a red reset button.

8. Print the selection

Open printing with the print button and choose All pages. When a selection is active, you can choose between Current selection – only the pages in the selection, preselected – and Entire session, which prints everything and ignores the selection.

The Simple range and Custom pages options are not available when a selection is active – the selection then decides what is printed. The rest of printing (reason, recipient, watermark) works as in the Getting started guide.

Good to know: printing a selection prints the pages as they are in the archive. If you have rotated a page or changed brightness and contrast, that is not included here – it only is with the Active page print method.

9. Remove the selection

Click the red button at the top of the thumbnail pane. All pages come back. You can also open the workspace, choose Add all, Save and Leave.

Short checklist

  • A green Save button means you have unsaved changes.
  • A red selection button in the toolbar means pages are hidden.
  • Current selection prints what you chose, Entire session prints everything.

Compare two pages

Click the compare button in the toolbar (Enable compare mode). The page you are looking at moves to the left with a blue frame, and a second page is shown on the right with an orange frame. To swap the right-hand page: right-click a thumbnail and choose Show to right.

One of the pages is always active – the one you last clicked in. The toolbar and the keyboard control the active page, and the frames around the buttons show which one: blue for left, orange for right.

If you print with the Active page method while comparing, you can choose to include only the active page or Both compare pages. The pages are printed exactly as you see them – rotation, brightness and contrast are included, just as when you look at a single page. With every other print method, the pages are printed as they are in the archive.

Controlling the other page without switching

Hold Shift while you click a button or use the keyboard, and the command applies to the page that is not active. The frames change colour while you hold Shift, so you can see what will happen.

Arrow Left and Arrow Right switch the active page. Arrow Left when the left page is already active ends the comparison – so does the compare button, or Close comparison in the right-click menu on the right-hand page.

Document metadata

Right-click a thumbnail or the page and choose Show document metadata. You see the information the system that opened ODV sent with the document – for example ID, name, department, date, number of pages and document ID. Which fields exist depends on that system. The information is for display only and cannot be changed in ODV.

All documents in one table

The Metadata table button in the metadata dialog shows the whole session as a table – one document per row. Handy when you want to see at a glance which departments and dates are included.

Sharper PDF pages

PDF pages are already shown in print quality. If you need to see a detail even more sharply: click HQ in the toolbar (Increase PDF page resolution). The page is redrawn at double resolution and the button turns red. It applies only to that page.

HQ is grey for scanned pages (TIFF/JPEG), because they are already shown exactly as sharp as they were scanned. Hover over the button to see why.

Custom size and default zoom

The 1:X button (Custom size) shows the page at a size you choose yourself – a share of the window width. The arrow next to it opens the settings. There you also choose which zoom every new session should start with (Default zoom/size); your organization decides the default. ODV remembers your choices.

The toolbar button by button

  • Print opens printing. The arrow next to it sets the default, Active page or All pages.
  • Selection opens the selection workspace. Red when a selection is active.
  • Page navigation: first, previous, next, last, and a box for the page number.
  • Zoom: out, in, percentage box, 1:1, fit to screen, fit to width.
  • 1:X: custom size. Compare: two pages side by side. Rotate: a quarter turn.
  • HQ: sharper PDF page. Brightness, contrast and reset.
  • Theme, language, help. The light on the help button: green = all is well, yellow = the computer is low on memory, red = error.

Dark theme

The theme menu at the far right has Normal, Light and Dark. In the dark theme the interface turns dark while the document page is shown as usual.

Support information

Turn to your local support first – they know how ODV is installed and set up in your organization, and can pass the case on to the supplier. Two things make it much easier to help you: the version and the diagnostics file. Both are under the help menu → About OpenDocViewer.

Good to include in the ticket:

  • Version and build ID from About OpenDocViewer.
  • The diagnostics file (Download diagnostics JSON). It contains technical information about ODV and the browser – no documents.
  • When it happened (date and time), what you did and which browser you used.
  • The text under Technical details if an error box was shown.

Do not send personal data. Do not attach screenshots or printouts that show real documents, names or ID numbers, unless your organization has explicitly asked for them.

When the session has expired

The box The documents could not be shown correctly has a Technical details link. Expand it to see which pages could not be fetched and why. Include that in the ticket.

New in version 2.8

  • The selection workspace replaces the old selection tab – see the guide Selection.
  • The print button has an arrow for the default scope, and the dialog has a new layout.
  • Custom size (1:X) and a choice of default zoom.
  • PDF pages in print quality, with HQ for an even sharper view.
  • Metadata from the right-click menu.
  • Page change when scrolling with the mouse wheel.
  • Reset session when the document links have expired.
  • Ctrl+P opens ODV's printing. Rotation is on Ctrl+Arrow Left/Right.

Short checklist

  • Wait until the page counter is no longer striped before you print.
  • Check the reason and the scope in the print dialog before you click Print.
  • If you get the expired-session box: click Reset session.
