Selection – choose the pages that are shown and printed

With a selection you decide which pages ODV shows and prints. You always start with all pages and remove the ones you do not want. It is handy when a session contains many documents but you only need a few pages.

This guide belongs with Getting started with OpenDocViewer. The pictures show a made-up test document, in the Swedish interface; the button names here are the English ones. Download the guide as PDF.

Important: a selection hides pages in ODV and controls what is printed. The original documents in the archive are never affected.

1. Open the selection workspace

Click the selection button in the toolbar – the one with a list and a tick, directly to the right of the print button (tooltip: Manage the pages shown and printed). The Manage selection workspace opens over the whole window.

Two modes: Documents and Pages
  • Documents is the default. Pages are kept together per document. You can remove and add pages and move them within their document.
  • Pages drops the grouping. Pages from different documents can be mixed in any order you like – for example before printing in a particular order.

2. Remove pages

  1. Mark the pages in the right panel that should not be included. Click one page at a time.
  2. Click Remove selected – the arrow ‹ between the panels. The pages lose their tick in the left panel.
  3. To remove everything and start from scratch: click Remove all («).
Faster ways to mark and remove
  • Shift+click marks every page between two clicks. Ctrl+click adds or removes a single page from the marking.
  • Drag a rectangle over several pages to mark them all at once.
  • Double-click a page in the left panel to add or remove it directly.
  • You do not need to open the workspace at all for single pages: right-click a thumbnail or the page in the main view and choose Hide this page or Hide document. The Delete key hides the page you are looking at, Ctrl+Delete the whole document.

3. Add pages back

Changed your mind? Mark the pages in the left panel and click Add selected (›), or Add all (») to get everything back. The Undo and Redo buttons in the toolbar take back one step at a time.

4. Change the order

Drag a page in the right panel to a new place. In Documents mode you can move pages within their document; in Pages mode you can mix freely. The order in the selection becomes the order of the printout.

5. Take a closer look at a page

Hover over a page and click the eye. The page is shown large, with arrows to browse to the next one. A green edge means the page is included in the selection, and the button at the top adds or removes the page directly. Esc closes.

6. Save or leave

The Save button turns green as soon as you have changed something. Click it when you are done, and then Leave to get back to the document. If you leave without saving, you get a question.

7. How to tell that a selection is active

Back in the document, the selection button is red, the page counter only counts the selection's pages, and the thumbnail pane only shows the pages that are included. At the top of the pane there is a red reset button.

8. Print the selection

Open printing with the print button and choose All pages. When a selection is active, you can choose between Current selection – only the pages in the selection, preselected – and Entire session, which prints everything and ignores the selection.

The Simple range and Custom pages options are not available when a selection is active – the selection then decides what is printed. The rest of printing (reason, recipient, watermark) works as in the Getting started guide.

Good to know: printing a selection prints the pages as they are in the archive. If you have rotated a page or changed brightness and contrast, that is not included here – it only is with the Active page print method.

9. Remove the selection

Click the red button at the top of the thumbnail pane. All pages come back. You can also open the workspace, choose Add all, Save and Leave.

Short checklist

  • A green Save button means you have unsaved changes.
  • A red selection button in the toolbar means pages are hidden.
  • Current selection prints what you chose, Entire session prints everything.
